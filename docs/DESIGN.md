# Easy Eyes design direction

An eyewear fitting table, treated as a gallery. The signature is a large, quiet frame silhouette that reveals an editorial image plane, with commerce controls set like precise gallery captions. Motion follows the act of looking: a restrained reveal, a clean image change, and immediate feedback for purchase actions. No ornamental 3D scene is needed without real product photography or geometry.

Tokens: ink `#111111`, chalk `#F3EFE7`, paper `#FFFFFF`, graphite `#5E5C57`, olive `#6D745F`. Display: Cormorant Garamond for editorial statements. UI: Manrope for navigation, details and forms. The EASY EYES mark uses the UI face with custom tracking. This stays within the brief's palette while reserving one expressive serif moment for campaign copy.

Desktop: asymmetric two-column editorial sections, generous negative space and large media. Mobile: one image plane, legible captions and direct browse actions. Focus, reduced motion and text contrast take precedence over animation.

The UI/UX skill returned a glass material system and card showcase for the broad query; these were rejected because they conflict with the supplied editorial brief. Its focus visibility and responsive guidance were retained.
